# The Short-Form Video Masterclass
### YouTube Shorts & Instagram Reels for the Indian Audience

**Course Duration:** 10 Hours (Intensive Masterclass)
**Format:** 6 Intensive Modules · Live Classes in Celoris Classrooms + Lifetime Session Recordings + ₹12,500 Bonus Pack
**Target Audience:** Content creators, small business owners, students, freelancers, and marketers who want to build an audience using short-form vertical video in the Indian market

---

## Course Outcomes
By the end of this course, a student will be able to:
- Ideate, script, shoot, and edit scroll-stopping Shorts/Reels using just a smartphone
- Understand how the YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels algorithms behave for Indian audiences specifically
- Build a content calendar around Indian trends, festivals, and regional language opportunities
- Grow a channel/page from zero to monetizable following
- Turn views into income via brand deals, affiliate, YouTube Shorts Fund/ads, and their own products/services

---

## MODULE 1 — Foundations & Mindset (1.5 Hours)

**1.1 Why Short-Form Video, Why Now (India Context)**
- Smartphone + cheap data revolution in India; regional language internet users
- YouTube Shorts vs Instagram Reels vs Moj/Josh — where Indian attention actually lives
- Case studies: Indian creators who blew up using only Shorts/Reels

**1.2 Choosing Your Niche for the Indian Market**
- High-demand niches in India: finance/education (edutainment), comedy/skits, food, fitness, desi lifestyle, tech in Hindi/regional languages, devotional content, agriculture/rural creators
- Language strategy: Hindi, Hinglish, English, or regional (Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Punjabi)
- Niche validation using trending searches and competitor mapping

**1.3 Setting Up Your Creator Toolkit**
- Budget smartphone setup (lighting, mic, tripod under ₹2000)
- Free/cheap apps: CapCut, InShot, VN, Canva, Alight Motion
- Setting up YouTube Shorts channel & Instagram Reels-first profile correctly

**Module 1 Assignment:** Define your niche, audience persona, and record your first unedited 15-second video.

---

## MODULE 2 — Ideation & High-Retention Scripting (1.5 Hours)

**2.1 The Hook Formula (First 1.5 Seconds Rule)**
- Pattern interrupts, curiosity gaps, bold claims, visual hooks
- Hooks that work specifically for Indian audiences (relatability, desi humor, "sasta/accha/tikau" value hooks)

**2.2 Scripting for 15–60 Second Retention**
- The 3-Act micro-structure: Hook → Value/Story → Payoff/CTA
- Writing in conversational Hindi/Hinglish that doesn't sound scripted
- Using trending audio + text overlays to script silently (for reach without voiceover)

**2.3 Finding Ideas on Repeat**
- Trend-jacking: festivals (Diwali, Holi, cricket season, exam season, wedding season)
- Using YouTube/Instagram trending pages, hashtag research, competitor remixing
- Batch ideation: generating 30 ideas in 30 minutes

**Module 2 Assignment:** Write 10 scripts using the hook formula; pick 3 to produce.

---

## MODULE 3 — Shooting Like a Pro on a Smartphone (2 Hours)

**3.1 Camera & Composition Basics**
- Vertical framing (9:16), rule of thirds, eye-level vs low angle for authority/comedy
- Natural light setup (India-specific: shooting near windows, avoiding midday harsh light)

**3.2 Audio That Doesn't Ruin Your Video**
- Clip-on mic recommendations (budget to premium)
- Recording clean audio in noisy Indian environments (traffic, family, fans)

**3.3 B-Roll & Multi-Shot Techniques**
- Shooting 5–7 shots for one Reel to keep retention high
- Using props, locations, and outfit changes for production value on a budget

**Module 3 Assignment:** Shoot one full Reel/Short using at least 4 different shots.

---

## MODULE 4 — Editing for Retention & Virality (2 Hours)

**4.1 Editing Fundamentals (CapCut/VN Workflow)**
- Jump cuts, speed ramping, zoom punches to maintain attention
- Adding captions (critical for India — many watch on mute/low data/public places)
- Trending transitions and effects that are currently working

**4.2 Sound Design & Music Selection**
- Using trending audio strategically (timing your post to catch a trend early)
- Original audio vs trending audio — when to use which
- Royalty-free/commercial-safe music sources for business accounts

**4.3 Thumbnails, Titles & Cover Frames (YouTube Shorts specific)**
- Writing titles that work with YouTube search + Shorts feed simultaneously
- Cover frame selection for Shorts shelf placement

**Module 4 Assignment:** Fully edit and publish 3 Reels/Shorts with captions, trending audio, and a strong CTA.

---

## MODULE 5 — Shorts & Reels Algorithm & Growth (1.5 Hours)

**5.1 How the Algorithms Actually Work**
- YouTube Shorts: watch time %, swipe-away rate, loop rate
- Instagram Reels: shares, saves, watch-through rate, and the "sends per reach" metric
- Posting frequency & timing for Indian time zones and peak usage hours

**5.2 Reading Your Analytics**
- Key metrics to track weekly: average view duration, retention graph, traffic sources
- Diagnosing why a Reel/Short flopped (hook, pacing, audio, or targeting)

**5.3 Community Building & Cross-Platform Strategy**
- Repurposing one Short across YouTube, Instagram, and Moj/Josh/Snack Video
- Using comments, polls, and duets/stitches to build a loyal Indian audience
- Collaborating with micro-creators in your niche/city

**Module 5 Assignment:** Publish daily for 7 days; submit an analytics screenshot report with observations.

---

## MODULE 6 — Monetization & Brand Deals (1.5 Hours)

**6.1 Platform Monetization**
- YouTube Shorts ad revenue/Partner Program eligibility for Indian creators
- Instagram Reels bonuses/brand partnership tools (subject to availability)

**6.2 Brand Deals & Sponsorships (India Playbook)**
- Building a media kit for Indian brands (rate cards in ₹, engagement-based pricing)
- Where Indian brands find creators (agencies, Cameo-style platforms, DMs, hashtag outreach)
- Negotiation basics and avoiding common scams targeting new Indian creators

**6.3 Beyond Ads: Building Your Own Income**
- Selling digital products, courses, or services to your Shorts/Reels audience
- Affiliate marketing (Amazon Associates India, Meesho, etc.)
- Turning a Shorts audience into a long-form YouTube or community (WhatsApp channel, Telegram) asset

**6.4 Capstone Project**
- Build a 30-day content calendar for your niche
- Present a full case study: 1 niche, 1 audience persona, 5 produced Reels/Shorts, and growth results
