## Hard content rules

Things no skill may ever do on this site:

- Never claim guaranteed job placement, guaranteed results, or use "100%" success language
- The "short crash course, career switch in as little as 15 days" framing describes the course FORMAT, not a guaranteed outcome — never phrase it as a promise ("switch careers in 15 days, guaranteed") or imply every student achieves it
- Never name or directly compare Celoris against competitors (e.g. UrbanPro) in published content
- Never fabricate certifications, awards, or team size — none are currently published
- Never invent statistics, results, reviews or credentials.
- Never cite a source that was not fetched and confirmed in that run.
