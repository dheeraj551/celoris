Digital Marketing Course in Delhi

Looking for a digital marketing course in Delhi that's more than theory? Here's the short version: we teach the same core skills every good institute teaches — SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, content, analytics — but you learn them by running real ad campaigns with your own budget and your own numbers, not by reading a case study of someone else's win. Classes run online, so it doesn't matter whether you're commuting from Pitampura, Laxmi Nagar, Dwarka, South Delhi, or further out in the NCR.

Book a free demo on WhatsApp →

Digital marketing isn't magic — it's just marketing

Digital marketing isn't some separate discipline with its own secret rules. It's the same strategy any product or service has always needed to sell — understand who's buying, put the right message in front of them, and measure what actually moves them — just run through digital channels instead of a shopfront or a cold call.

That's why our courses aren't built around case studies of other people's campaigns. You get hands-on training on live projects, running real Google and Meta ad campaigns during the course, so you're learning from your own real data — what actually happened when you spent the budget and wrote the ad — instead of studying a slide deck of somebody else's results.

What you'll actually learn

The curriculum covers the full stack a working digital marketer needs, not just one channel in isolation:

  1. Digital Marketing Fundamentals
  2. Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
  3. Google Ads (Search, Display and YouTube)
  4. Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram)
  5. Social Media Marketing (organic)
  6. Content Marketing & Copywriting
  7. Analytics & Reporting
  8. Freelancing & Agency Skills (bonus module)

Every track includes 10+ practice assignments, live ad campaign experience, a completion certificate, and lifetime WhatsApp support if you get stuck after the course ends.

What does a digital marketing course in Delhi actually cost?

Celoris runs three fee tracks depending on how much depth and 1-on-1 time you want, starting at ₹2,500 with no hidden charges added later. Pick based on your actual starting point rather than the most expensive option — a beginner doesn't need 1-on-1 mentoring, and a working professional usually doesn't need the 4-week Basic pace.

Transparent pricing matters more in this market than it should have to — it's common to see a course advertised at one number and then have extra modules, certificates, or "advanced" content pitched separately once you're already enrolled. What you see above is what you pay.

Does it come with placement support?

We don't promise guaranteed placement, and any course in Delhi that does is a claim worth questioning. What we do offer is real, practical experience: by the end of the course, you'll have actually run live ad campaigns with your own results to show, which is what most entry-level marketing roles and freelance clients actually want to see — not a certificate on its own.

Our trainers have managed ₹50L+ in real ad spends for Delhi NCR clients, so the campaign practice you're doing is based on current, real market conditions rather than a static syllabus written a few years ago.

Is this the right digital marketing course for you?

If you're a student or beginner in Delhi with no prior marketing background, the Basic track gets you through the fundamentals in 4 weeks. If you're already working and want to add digital skills to your resume without quitting your job, Advanced runs over 8 weeks alongside your schedule. If you're a freelancer or business owner who wants direct, 1-on-1 attention on your own product or service, Mastery is built around that.

What we won't do is tell you we're the only option in Delhi worth considering — there are several good institutes here, and the right fit depends on whether you learn better from live campaign practice (what we focus on) or from a more theory-first, classroom-style approach.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cost of a digital marketing course in Delhi?

At Celoris, the Basic track is ₹2,500 for 4 weeks, Advanced is ₹4,999 for 8 weeks, and 1-on-1 Mastery is ₹8,000 for 8 weeks — no hidden charges added after you enroll. Prices across Delhi vary a lot by institute and course depth, so compare what's actually included, not just the headline number.

Which institute is best for digital marketing in Delhi?

That depends on whether you want live campaign practice or theory, and whether you're a beginner or already working. Celoris focuses specifically on hands-on practice — you run real Google and Meta ad campaigns during the course, learning from your own live data rather than a case study of someone else's results.

Which course is best in digital marketing?

The best course is the one that matches your actual starting point and goal, not the one with the longest module list. Celoris runs three tracks — a 4-week Basic for beginners and students, an 8-week Advanced for working professionals, and an 8-week 1-on-1 Mastery for freelancers and business owners — each covering the same core channels at a different depth.

Is there a free digital marketing course in Delhi?

Celoris's paid tracks start at ₹2,500, but if you want to try the platform first, Celoris's free tools (Video Studio, Image Studio, and AI Explorer) are open with no credit card required. They're not a substitute for the structured course, but they're a genuine way to get a feel for how we teach before you commit.
