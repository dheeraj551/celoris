Digital Marketing Course in Noida

Celoris runs a digital marketing course in Noida built around one idea: show you exactly what you're paying for before you pay for it. That means published pricing with no hidden fees, batches capped at 5 students, and live ad-campaign practice instead of a stack of recorded videos — available in person across Noida, Greater Noida and Ghaziabad, or fully online.

What Do You Get in Celoris's Digital Marketing Course in Noida?

You get a structured path through SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, organic social media, content marketing and analytics, taught by trainers who run real client campaigns, not just theory. The course is split into 8 modules — Fundamentals, SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, Social Media Marketing, Content Marketing & Copywriting, Analytics & Reporting, and a bonus Freelancing & Agency Skills module for anyone who wants to work independently after finishing.

Every plan includes 10+ practice assignments, hands-on work inside live ad campaigns, a Celoris Course Completion Certificate, and lifetime WhatsApp support for questions that come up after you're done — WhatsApp: +91 90847 18101.

How Much Does a Digital Marketing Course Cost in Noida?

Celoris's digital marketing course in Noida runs ₹2,500 for the 4-week Basic plan up to ₹8,000 for the 8-week Mastery (1-on-1) plan, with no hidden charges on any tier. All three tiers include the same core inclusions — practice assignments, live campaign work and a certificate — so the price difference mainly buys you a smaller batch and more direct trainer time. Here's the full breakdown:

Tier Price Duration Built for
Basic ₹2,500 4 weeks Beginners & students
Advanced ₹4,999 8 weeks Working professionals
Mastery (1-on-1) ₹8,000 8 weeks Freelancers & business owners

Prices as of September 2026 — WhatsApp +91 90847 18101 to confirm current rates before enrolling. All three plans include the same core promise: 10+ practice assignments, live campaign experience, a completion certificate, and lifetime WhatsApp support — the difference between tiers is batch size, duration and how much 1-on-1 time you get with a trainer.

Is There a Digital Marketing Course Near You in Noida?

Yes — Celoris runs in-person and small-group batches across Noida Sectors 18, 22, 37, 44, 50, 56, 62, 63, 76, 93, 100, 120, 125, 135 and 137, plus Greater Noida (Knowledge Park I–V, Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta, Omega and Greater Noida West's Gaur City, Supertech Eco Village and Amrapali Silicon City) and Ghaziabad (Indirapuram, Vaishali, Vasundhara, Crossings Republik). Trainers offer home-visit sessions across all of these, or you can join fully online via Zoom or Google Meet if you're outside the area, or just prefer to learn from home.

If you're weighing a local, in-person option against a national online platform, location genuinely matters here: running a live ad campaign is easier to troubleshoot face-to-face the first time you try it, which is the main reason Celoris still offers home visits instead of going online-only.

Will This Course Actually Get You a Job in Digital Marketing?

No course can honestly guarantee you a job, and Celoris doesn't claim to — what it offers instead is campaign work you can actually show an employer or client. As of today, there were 986 open digital marketing roles listed on Internshala for Delhi NCR alone — a live count that moves daily, but a reasonable sense of how much hiring activity exists in this specific market right now.

Whether that turns into a job for you depends on what you can prove you've actually done, which is the entire reason the course is structured around live campaigns instead of quizzes: a portfolio of real ad spend you managed carries more weight in an interview than a certificate for videos you watched.

What Makes Celoris Different From Other Digital Marketing Courses in Noida?

Batches are capped at 5 students with real client ad-spend behind the practice work, pricing is published upfront with nothing added later, and a trainer can come to you — that's the actual difference, not a claim about being better than any specific competitor. There's a simple reason this page doesn't run through a feature-by-feature scorecard against other named providers in Noida: in digital marketing specifically, almost everyone ends up writing the same list of "why choose us" points, and a course built to copy that formula just blends into the same noise as everyone else's.

Dheeraj Kushwaha, Celoris's founder, puts it plainly: in a field where hundreds of people are already writing about the same topic, doing it the way everyone else already does guarantees you get overshadowed by whoever's biggest — the only way to actually get noticed is to build your own way of doing things, even if it's less conventional, rather than following the path a bigger, already-successful business laid down first. That's the thinking behind why this course runs small, in-person batches with real campaign budgets instead of copying the recorded-video, large-cohort format most competitors default to.

Do You Need a Certification, or Just Practical Skills?

A Celoris Course Completion Certificate comes with every plan, but it's not the reason to enrol — the thing that actually helps in a hiring conversation is a small portfolio of real campaigns you ran yourself. Add the certificate to LinkedIn or your resume as a supporting credential, not the headline.

This matters most if you're a complete beginner deciding between a short, practical track and a longer certification-heavy one: the Basic plan is built for exactly that — no prior marketing background required, starting from fundamentals, with the practical work (not the paperwork) doing the heavy lifting.

How Long Is the Course, and Can You Learn Online or In Person?

The course runs 4 to 8 weeks depending on the plan, and you can take it fully online via Zoom or Google Meet, in person at Celoris, or with a trainer visiting your home across Noida — all three formats cover the same curriculum and modules. The 4-week Basic track suits someone who wants the fundamentals fast; the 8-week Advanced and Mastery tracks add Google Ads, Meta Ads and full analytics for anyone going deeper or planning to freelance afterward.

Format doesn't change what you're taught, only how you attend — pick online if flexibility matters more, or in-person/home-visit if you specifically want someone watching your first live campaign over your shoulder.
