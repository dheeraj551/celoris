# How the Celoris Job Center Ranking System Works: Turn Your Time and Skills Into Real Job Offers

Most job portals treat you the same on day one as they do a year later. You create a profile, you scroll listings, and unless you already have the "right" resume, nothing about actually *using* the platform changes your odds.

Celoris's Job Center works differently. It's built around a ranking system that notices two things most platforms ignore: how consistently you show up, and how many of your skills you've actually proven — not just claimed on a resume. Do both, and better opportunities open up automatically.

Here's exactly how it works.

## The Core Idea: XP, Levels, and What They Unlock

Every candidate on the Job Center has an XP total and a Level, from 1 to 5. Your level isn't cosmetic — it's the gate between the open **Public Jobs** portal and the higher-paying **Certified Roles** portal (₹150k – ₹340k+), which is only visible to candidates who've proven themselves.

| Level | Title | XP Needed | What It Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Novice Explorer | 0 | Apply to all Public entry roles, take Beginner assessments, set up job alerts |
| 2 | Skilled Practitioner | 250 | Public mid-level roles, priority application visibility, Intermediate exams unlock |
| 3 | Verified Specialist | 600 | Certified High-End Portal Tier 1 (₹120k+), Verified Skill Badge on your profile |
| 4 | Senior Authority | 1,200 | Certified Lead & Staff roles (₹180k+), direct hiring-manager inbox priority |
| 5 | Elite Principal Architect | 2,500 | Executive & Principal roles (₹250k+), VIP salary negotiation support |

Two things move you up that table: **showing up consistently**, and **proving your skills**.

## 1. Loyalty XP — You're Rewarded for Actually Using the Platform

This is the part most job portals skip entirely. On Celoris, time you genuinely spend engaged with the Job Center — browsing roles, checking your progression, working toward an exam — earns you steady XP in the background, up to a fair daily cap.

It's deliberately not designed to reward a tab sitting open and forgotten. The XP only ticks while you're actually there, actively working the platform, not idling in the background — which is exactly what makes it meaningful. Candidates who check in regularly and stay engaged climb levels naturally, long before they take their first exam.

Applying to a role adds a small XP bump too, so every serious application moves you forward, not just exams.

## 2. Exam XP — The Bigger Jumps, and the Badges That Open Certified Roles

The second, larger source of XP is Celoris's **anti-cheat verified skill exams** — timed assessments (MCQ + real scenario questions) proctored with tab-switch detection, focus tracking, and an Honor Score that reflects how clean your attempt was.

Pass one, and two things happen at once:

- You get a meaningful XP jump toward your next level.
- You earn a **Verified Badge** — a cryptographically hashed credential (`SV-2026-XXXX-XXXX` format) tied to that specific skill, visible on your public candidate profile.

Badges aren't just for show. Certified Roles list `requiredBadges` — specific credentials a candidate must hold before the role even becomes visible to apply for. Employers browsing the Certified portal are filtering by real, verified proof of skill, not by keywords on a resume. That's the whole point of anti-cheat proctoring: a badge only means something if an employer can trust it wasn't faked.

As of this writing, exams are live across skills including Full-Stack React & TypeScript, Cloud AI & Prompt Systems Engineering, and Social Media Graphic Design & Motion Video Editing — with more added as new Certified Roles go up.

## Why Build It This Way?

Because the two things that actually predict whether someone succeeds in a role — consistency and real ability — are exactly the two things most hiring processes struggle to measure honestly. A resume can claim five years of "advanced Excel." A Verified Badge means you sat a proctored exam and passed it.

The ranking system just makes that visible, and rewards it in real time: stay engaged, and you level up. Prove a skill, and the roles that need it unlock.
