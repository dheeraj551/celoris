### 5 Comprehensive Chapters (34+ Topics)

#### Chapter 1: Light - Reflection and Refraction (8 hours)
1. Reflection of Light - Laws, Regular vs. Diffuse Reflection
2. Spherical Mirrors - Concave and Convex Mirrors, Key Terms
3. Ray Diagrams for Mirrors - 6 Cases for Concave, Convex Mirror
4. Mirror Formula & Magnification - (1/v + 1/u = 1/f), (m = -v/u)
5. Refraction of Light - Laws, Refractive Index (n = c/v)
6. Spherical Lenses - Concave and Convex Lenses
7. Ray Diagrams for Lenses - 6 Cases for Convex, Concave Lens
8. Lens Formula & Power - (1/v - 1/u = 1/f), (P = 1/f)

#### Chapter 2: The Human Eye and the Colourful World (5 hours)
1. The Human Eye - Structure, Accommodation, Near/Far Point
2. Defects of Vision - Myopia, Hypermetropia, Presbyopia
3. Refraction through a Prism - Prism Angle, Deviation
4. Dispersion of Light - VIBGYOR Spectrum
5. Atmospheric Effects - Twinkling, Scattering, Blue Sky, Red Sunset

#### Chapter 3: Electricity (7 hours)
1. Electric Current & Charge - (I = Q/t), Coulomb, Ampere
2. Potential Difference - (V = W/Q), Volt, Voltmeter
3. Ohm's Law and Resistance - (V = IR), Ohm (Ω)
4. Resistivity - (ρ = RA/L), Conductors vs. Insulators
5. Combination of Resistors - Series and Parallel Circuits
6. Heating Effect - Joule's Law (H = I²Rt), Fuse
7. Electric Power - (P = VI = I²R = V²/R), kWh

#### Chapter 4: Magnetic Effects of Electric Current (8 hours)
1. Magnetic Field & Field Lines - Properties, Bar Magnet
2. Field due to Current-Carrying Conductor - Patterns
3. Direction of Field - Right-Hand Thumb Rule
4. Force on Conductor - (F = BIL), Fleming's Left-Hand Rule
5. Electric Motor - Principle, Construction, Working
6. Electromagnetic Induction - Faraday's Discovery
7. Electric Generator - AC vs. DC, Fleming's Right-Hand Rule
8. Domestic Electric Circuits - Live, Neutral, Earth Wire, Safety

#### Chapter 5: Sources of Energy (6 hours)
1. Good Source of Energy - Characteristics, Ideal Fuel
2. Conventional Sources - Fossil Fuels, Thermal, Hydro Power
3. Non-Conventional Sources - Solar, Wind, Biomass, Nuclear
4. Environmental Consequences - Sustainability, Renewable Energy
