If you've ever tried to learn a new skill online in India, you already know the drill.

You search for "Excel trainer near me" or "learn digital marketing online," and within minutes your phone won't stop ringing. Five different "counsellors" call you, each with a slightly different price, a slightly different pitch, and a deadline that always seems to expire "today only." Most platforms lure you in with a free trial, a free consultation call, or a "no-cost demo class" — and then the moment you're interested, the real pitch begins. Hidden charges. Bundled packages you didn't ask for. Renewal fees buried in the fine print.

We don't do that. When we say **no hidden charges**, we mean it — what you see is what you pay, and in a lot of cases, what you get is completely free to begin with.

## No Surprise Calls From "Counsellors"

You know the ones. The calls that start with "just a quick 2-minute chat" and turn into a 40-minute sales pitch, sometimes from three or four different people trying to upsell you before you've even decided if the platform is right for you.

At Celoris, you find a trainer, you talk to *them* — not a middleman whose only job is to close the sale. No scripts, no upsells, no pressure. Just you and the person who's actually going to teach you.
