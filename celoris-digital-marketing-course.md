## Course Overview

Digital Marketing is one of the fastest-growing skill demands in India today. Every business — from local shops to startups to enterprises — needs someone who can build their online presence, run ads that convert, and grow their brand on social media.

This course takes you from zero to job-ready, covering everything from SEO fundamentals to running live Meta and Google ad campaigns. You won't just learn concepts — you'll build a portfolio of real campaigns you can show employers or use for your own business.

**Perfect for:** Students starting their career, working professionals looking to upskill, business owners who want to market themselves, and freelancers looking to add a high-demand skill.

## Curriculum

### Module 1: Digital Marketing Foundations
- What is digital marketing and why it matters in 2026
- Understanding the marketing funnel (awareness → consideration → conversion)
- Overview of all digital channels: SEO, social, paid ads, email, content

### Module 2: Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
- How search engines rank websites
- Keyword research (tools: Google Keyword Planner, Ubersuggest)
- On-page SEO: titles, meta descriptions, content structure
- Off-page SEO: backlinks, domain authority basics
- Local SEO for small businesses (Google Business Profile)

### Module 3: Social Media Marketing
- Platform-specific strategy: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn
- Content planning and posting calendars
- Organic growth tactics (hashtags, Reels, engagement)
- Community management basics

### Module 4: Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)
- Meta Ads Manager walkthrough
- Audience targeting and custom audiences
- Ad formats: image, video, carousel, Reels ads
- Budgeting and bidding strategies
- Reading ad performance metrics (CTR, CPC, ROAS)

### Module 5: Google Ads
- Search ads vs. display ads vs. YouTube ads
- Keyword bidding and Quality Score
- Campaign structure best practices
- Google Analytics basics for tracking performance

### Module 6: Content & Email Marketing
- Writing marketing copy that converts
- Basics of email marketing (Mailchimp/similar tools)
- Building a simple funnel: landing page → email → sale

### Module 7: Analytics & Reporting
- Reading data from Meta Ads Manager, Google Analytics
- Building simple client-ready performance reports
- Making data-driven decisions to improve campaigns

### Module 8: Capstone Project
- Plan and execute a real (or simulated) campaign end-to-end
- Present a portfolio piece: strategy + execution + results
- Get trainer feedback for portfolio/interview readiness

Absolutely — many students join specifically to market their own business rather than pursue a marketing job.
