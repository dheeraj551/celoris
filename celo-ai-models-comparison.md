## Why Multiple Models Instead of Just One?

Different AI models are built with different strengths. Some are optimized for speed — perfect for quick, everyday doubts. Others are built for deep reasoning — better suited for complex problem-solving, like debugging a Python script or working through an Excel formula that isn't behaving. A few specialize in coding, and some are excellent multilingual communicators — handy if you're more comfortable framing a question in Hindi or Hinglish.

Rather than force everyone into a one-size-fits-all box, Celo AI lets you pick the right tool for the job — for free.
