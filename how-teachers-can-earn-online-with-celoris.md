Classroom and one-on-one tuition will always have their place. But the demand for online learning has fundamentally reshaped how students find trainers and how trainers find income.

- **Reach beyond your neighborhood.** Your next student doesn't have to live in your locality — they could be anywhere in Delhi/NCR, or even further.

## The Real Problem Most Teachers Face

If you've tried teaching online before, or explored platforms to list yourself as a trainer, you've likely run into at least one of these:

1. **Per-lead charges** — you pay coins or credits just to see a student's contact details, whether or not they convert into a paying student.
2. **No control over pricing** — platforms dictate what you can charge.
3. **Delayed or unclear payments** — you teach first, and chase payment later.
4. **Low trust signals** — students can't easily verify if you're a genuine, experienced trainer.

These issues aren't your fault — they're structural problems with how most tutor marketplaces are built. This is exactly the gap Celoris was created to close.
